# SkyFlutter Dash

SkyFlutter Dash is an exciting, endless flying game playable on PC and mobile browsers—now optimized for **landscape mode on mobile**! Guide your bird through moving pipes, chase high scores, and enjoy vibrant backgrounds with family and friends.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Goal:**  
  Fly as far as you can by flapping your bird through gaps between vertical pipes.
- **Challenge:**  
  The pipes move across the city skyline. Time your flaps to avoid collisions and keep your bird airborne.
- **Score:**  
  Earn points for every pipe you pass. How high can you score?

---

## 🕹️ Controls

**On PC or Laptop:**
- Press the **Spacebar** or **Left Mouse Button** to flap upwards.

**On Mobile or Tablet (landscape only):**
- **Tap anywhere on the screen** to flap upwards.

**Tip:**  
Hold your device horizontally (landscape) for the best experience.  
If using your phone and the game asks you to rotate, do so for optimal play.

---

## ✨ Features

- Simple, responsive controls for all devices
- Endless gameplay with increasing difficulty
- Animated city backgrounds and smooth pipe movement
- Real-time score tracking
- Sound effects for extra fun (mute button included!)
- Free and open-source
